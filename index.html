How to read a card
  • Pass = Risk is OK with it. Caution = OK, but read the reason first. Blocked = Risk says no; it can't be added.
  • Mark is the option price per share (market price). Account-based cost and loss figures are hidden on the public page.
  • Volume is yesterday's full day vs a normal day (1.00× = normal). It isn't a live read.
  • News: green = news backs the trade, red = news goes against it, grey = no real news (X chatter only). "Old" means the read is over 18 hours old.
  • This public page is read-only: no orders, no approvals.
